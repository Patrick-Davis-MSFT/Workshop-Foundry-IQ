# G Coffee Shop Transaction 202307 to 202506

## About Dataset
This dataset provides a synthetically generated, comprehensive record of coffee shop transactions spanning July 2023 to June 2025. It is specifically designed to simulate the crucial period following the launch of a new customer membership program and mobile application, offering a unique lens into the evolving dynamics of customer engagement and purchasing behavior.

## Key Features & Purpose:
Simulated Loyalty Program Impact: The dataset distinguishes between member and non-member transactions, allowing for in-depth analysis of how a loyalty program influences purchase frequency, basket size, and product preferences.

Granular Transaction Data: Each record captures essential details such as unique Transaction ID, Customer ID, Product Name, Quantity, Unit Price, Total Amount, and precise Transaction Timestamp.

Temporal Analysis: Covering a full two-year period, the data enables time-series analysis to identify trends, seasonality, and the long-term effects of the membership launch.

Realistic Patterns: The underlying data generation methodology incorporates logic for varying customer activity, popular product distribution, and peak hour transactions, mimicking typical coffee shop operations.

## Potential Use Cases:
Customer Segmentation: Identify differences in purchasing habits between members and non-members.

Loyalty Program Effectiveness: Quantify the impact of the membership program on sales, customer retention, and average transaction value.

Sales Forecasting: Develop models to predict future sales based on historical patterns and customer segments.

Product Popularity & Inventory Management: Analyze best-selling items and optimize stock levels.

Time-Series Analysis: Explore daily, weekly, and monthly sales trends.

This dataset is ideal for data analysts, data scientists, and business intelligence professionals looking to practice and demonstrate skills in retail analytics, customer behavior analysis, and the evaluation of loyalty initiatives in a controlled, accessible setting.
